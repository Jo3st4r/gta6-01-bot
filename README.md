**Solmar Bay** — an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for
make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. Rico Vega is back in the coastal city of Solmar Bay,
and the Marlin crew runs the streets. Explore the city on foot and by car, steal cars,
dodge the police, and run five story missions to take the city back.

- A 2560×1920 coastal city: roads, traffic, pedestrians, a beach and a marina
- Cars to steal and drive, with a wanted level (0–5 stars) and police chases
- Five story missions: First Wheels, The Pickup, Marlin Heat, Shakedown, The Last Score
- Pistol combat, a HUD with a live minimap, health and cash
- Start screen, save to localStorage with autosave, synthesized WebAudio music and SFX
- Pure static files — no build step, no network requests, plays offline in desktop Chrome

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Controls: WASD / arrows to move and
drive, E to steal or exit a car, Space / J / click to shoot, P to pause, Enter to start.
